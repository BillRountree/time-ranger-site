Time Ranger Privacy Policy

Invisible Game Design · Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the VR game Time Ranger (“the game”), on Meta Quest and on Steam, collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can: the game has no accounts of its own and we never sell your information.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhyWhere it goes
Gameplay analytics (can be turned off, see section 3): your head position and gaze direction in the game, controller movement and button use, which levels and sections you play and how they end (enemies hit, deaths, challenges completed), estimated arm length and standing height, play-area size, headset model and hardware details, framerate, battery level and Wi-Fi signal strength, and a device identifier. To find out which parts of the game are too hard, confusing or broken, and to fix performance problems. Cognitive3D, our analytics provider.
Your Meta or Steam username and user ID, your scores and the achievements you unlock To put your scores on the online leaderboards, show other players' scores, and award achievements. Meta, through the Meta Horizon platform (Quest), or Valve, through Steam (PC). Your username, scores and achievements are visible to other players as set by your Meta or Steam privacy settings.
Purchases and entitlement To check that you own the game and to unlock anything you buy in it. Meta (on Quest) or Valve (on Steam) handles all payments; we never see your payment details.
Settings and progress (unlocked levels, best scores, options) To remember your progress and preferences. Stored on your device. On Meta Quest, if backup is on for your account, Meta's cloud backup keeps a copy so your progress comes back after a reinstall or on a new headset. Purchase records and analytics queues are never included in the backup.
Body tracking (Meta Quest version only): the position of your arms and elbows, as estimated by your headset's body tracking. So the watch and bracer on your in-game hands follow your real forearm. Nowhere. It is used on your headset while you play and is not stored or sent to us or anyone else.

The game does not record your voice or microphone, does not access headset camera images, and does not collect your real name, email address, contacts or precise location. Our service providers may see your IP address when the game connects to them, as any online service does.

2. Who we share it with

  • Cognitive3D processes the gameplay analytics for us. See the Cognitive3D privacy policy.
  • Meta provides the platform features (leaderboards, purchases, entitlement). See the Meta Quest privacy policy.
  • Valve sells and delivers the Steam version. See the Steam privacy policy.

We do not sell or rent your information, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

3. Your choices

  • Turn analytics off: in the game, open Settings > PRIVACY and set SHARE PLAY DATA to OFF. Analytics stop straight away and the game remembers your choice.
  • Leaderboards and achievements: these use your Meta or Steam account; you can manage what is shown to others in your Meta account and Quest privacy settings, or your Steam profile privacy settings.
  • Delete your progress: in the game, open Settings and use RESET PROGRESS; this erases your progress and best scores and keeps your options. Uninstalling removes everything from the device; on Meta Quest, a cloud backup copy can be removed in your headset's backup settings.
  • Access or deletion requests: email us (section 6) to ask for a copy of, or deletion of, the analytics data linked to your device. Include your headset's model; we may ask for more details to find it.

4. How long we keep it

Gameplay analytics are kept for as long as they are useful for improving the game, and for no more than 24 months. Leaderboard entries stay up while the game's leaderboards run; Meta and Valve keep account data under their own policies.

To protect it, gameplay analytics are sent to Cognitive3D only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and are not linked to your name or email address; access to them is limited to the Time Ranger team. Everything else stays on your headset or with Meta and Valve under their own security practices.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Contact and changes

Questions or requests: social@invisiblegamedesign.com (Invisible Game Design).

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects what we collect, the game will tell you before it applies.
