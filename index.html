Step into the boots of the Time Ranger

Remember feeding coins into the light gun cabinet at the back of the arcade? Time Ranger brings that rush to VR: a fast, on-rails shooter where you chase outlaws through broken timelines with a pistol in each hand.

No inventory. No menus to fiddle with. Just you, the next wave, and the clock. Each level drops you into a new era with its own enemies, music and a boss waiting at the end.

  • Instant actionPut the headset on and you are shooting in seconds.
  • Short, intense runsSelf-contained levels built for 5 to 7 minute sessions.
  • Endless replayScores, medals, challenges and online leaderboards.

The story

Time is broken. Your brother is missing.

When a mysterious artifact crashes from the sky and fractures time, history starts to fall apart.

You are the Ranger: once a frontier lawman, now a time-bending gunslinger. The town's Sheriff, corrupted by the artifact's power, has kidnapped your brother, your deputy and closest ally.

To save him you will fight across fractured eras, through paradoxes, warped creatures and agents sworn to protect the timeline. Every showdown brings you closer to your brother, and to the moment that could set time right, or end it forever.

Who's building it

Two devs. No publisher. All heart.

Time Ranger is made by two industry veterans with over 30 years of combined experience on games like Shadowgun Legends, 8 Ball Pool and Angry Birds Action!. After getting caught up in the industry's layoffs, they teamed up to make the game they always wanted to play, and they are funding it themselves.

If you'd like to help: wishlist, tell a friend, and play the demo. Every bit of feedback makes the game better.
